## Libraries inside sharp's prebuilt binaries

Papeleria converts a piece's images with sharp (Apache License 2.0; Copyright 2013 Lovell Fuller and others). npm installs sharp with Papeleria, together with the prebuilt packages for the author's platform. They run only on the author's machine: nothing from them is in Papeleria's own package, and nothing from them is copied into a piece.
