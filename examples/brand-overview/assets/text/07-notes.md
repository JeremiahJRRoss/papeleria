The repository names Inter/Avenir and Fraunces/Times but does not load those fonts in the inspected source. This kit sets Poppins for display at weight 600, chosen because its constructed geometry matches the mark’s letterforms, and Inter for reading and UI at 18 px body, chosen for screen legibility; monospace remains the system stack for IDs and code. Both families are self-hosted under assets/fonts/ as unicode-range-gated latin and latin-ext woff2, about 170 KB in total, so the kit renders identically offline and from disk with no CDN and no third-party request. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits commercial use, embedding, and redistribution with the license retained.

Superseded history, not current guidance: RM-D04 used Georgia with Times New Roman for display and Arial/Helvetica for reading, and stated that no font files are packaged. Font binaries are now packaged, so that statement no longer describes the kit. Display weight moves from 400 to 600 and negative tracking eases from about -.075em to about -.03em, because tracking tuned for Georgia collapses a geometric sans.
